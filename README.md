# AI Use in Auditing: Audit Quality, Regulation and Professional Skepticism

Team presentation for MGTP 433, Issues in Practice and Regulation of Auditing, at the University of California, San Diego, Rady School of Management (Spring 2026).

## Overview

- How audit firms are adopting generative and agentic AI
- Evidence on AI and audit quality, including Law and Shen (2024), *Management Science*
- The PCAOB's evolving position and the regulatory timeline
- Why auditor judgment remains the most common inspection deficiency

**Argument:** AI raises the stakes for professional skepticism instead of lowering them.
